Founder

Uditha Harish

Uditha Harish is the founder of MoveMinds, a youth-led initiative dedicated to creating inclusive adaptive dance experiences for neurodiverse children.

Her dance journey began in 2015 through Bharatanatyam and ballet. Through movement and community service, she discovered the ability of dance to build confidence, encourage self-expression, and create belonging.

Uditha is also passionate about neuroscience, medicine, research, and service. She hopes to combine science and compassion to create solutions that support individuals with neurological and developmental differences.
