Our Story

Movement that builds belonging

MoveMinds was founded by Uditha Harish. Her journey began in 2015 through Bharatanatyam and ballet.

Through years of dance and volunteering experiences with neurodiverse children, she discovered that movement could be a powerful way to build confidence, creativity, communication, and connection.

MoveMinds was created to provide welcoming spaces where every child can move freely, express themselves, and feel valued.

Meet the founder
Our Mission

Confidence, creativity, connection

To empower neurodiverse children through adaptive dance and movement experiences that foster confidence, creativity, and meaningful connection.

Our Vision

Joyful movement for every child

A world where every neurodiverse child has access to inclusive, joyful movement opportunities.

Why Movement Matters

The joy of moving, together

Movement is more than dance. For neurodiverse children, it becomes a language of confidence, expression, and belonging.

01

Builds Confidence

Every small step forward becomes a moment of pride, helping children believe in what they can do.

02

Encourages Expression

Movement gives children a creative, non-verbal way to share how they feel and who they are.

03

Creates Connection

Shared movement builds friendships, trust, and a genuine sense of community for children and families.

04

Celebrates Individuality

Every mind moves differently, and MoveMinds honors each child's unique rhythm, pace, and personality.

Every child can move. Every child can shine.
